System audio access

Needed to hear the interview at all. macOS may show a one-time system prompt the first time you click this — better to see it now than during a call.

Screen recording access*

Required to start listening. The "Screenshot your code" button in the hint panel toolbar is always available, and macOS requires this permission before the app can run its own screenshot tool at all. Better to grant it now than to meet the system prompt during a call. You may need to restart Notepad after allowing it.

System Settings didn't open the right section?

    Make sure you toggle "Screen & System Audio Recording" — not the similarly-named "System Audio Recording Only" nearby. That's a different, unrelated permission this app doesn't use.
